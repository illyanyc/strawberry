# Market data

**Prefix:** `MD-`  ·  **Owners:** `marketdata` (backfill, streaming, bar building, gap repair, reconciliation, reference-data sync), `broker-gateway` (vendor connections)  ·  **Related:** [storage](storage.md) (ST-), [data and features](data-and-features.md) (DF-), [brokers](brokers.md) (BR-015, BR-016), D20, D27, [ADR-0022](../adr/0022-market-data-ingestion.md)

## Scope

Ingestion of bars, quotes, trades, corporate actions, and instrument reference data from Alpaca (through broker-gateway) and FMP: finality and availability timestamps, session alignment, gap detection and repair, rate limits, source health, and cross-source reconciliation. Storage mechanics are in [storage](storage.md); point-in-time read semantics in [data and features](data-and-features.md).

## Rules

| ID | Rule | Status | Code / tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| MD-001 | Historical backfill is a resumable, idempotent job: the request is split into chunks (≤ 100 symbols × a per-timeframe window: 7 days for 1m, 30 for 5m, 60 for 15m, 3 years for 1d) and each chunk is checkpointed in `marketdata.backfill_chunks` after its bars are durably in the lake; a restarted job skips finished chunks; re-running a job writes zero new rows; a caller-chosen job ID makes submission idempotent (startup jobs use IDs derived from their parameters). Vendor 1-hour bars are refused (they start on the clock hour, not 09:30). Bars are requested raw with Alpaca's `asof` = the window's last day so a recycled ticker never returns another company's history. | Implemented | `internal/ingest` (`Backfiller.Plan`, `RunJob`, `PGStore`), broker-gateway `alpaca.HistoricalBars`; `TestMD001BackfillIdempotentWithGapRepairAndTickerChange`, `TestMD001BackfillResumesFromCheckpoint`, `TestMD001SubmitWithIDIsIdempotentAndRejectsVendorHourly` |
| MD-002 | A live 1-minute bar becomes final `60 s` after its close (Alpaca's `updatedBars` window); until then it is published only as a partial bar (`md.bar.partial.*`, UI only, never stored). An update to a bar after it is final is a correction: a new revision in the lake (DF-007), a `md.bar.correction.*` event, and the cache overwritten; derived bars are rebuilt and corrected too. | Implemented | `internal/live` (`Pipeline.OnBar`, `OnUpdatedBar`, `Tick`); `TestMD002FinalityWindowAndCorrections` |
| MD-003 | Every bar's `available_at` = `ts_close` + the feed's publication delay (defaults: Alpaca 60 s, FirstRate 60 s, FMP daily 6 h; override with `TB_PUBLICATION_DELAYS`), and never earlier than the first time the platform saw it; historical backfill uses the same delay so live and backfilled bars agree (DF-002). | Implemented | `internal/ingest` (`Delays`, `Normalizer.Normalize`); `TestMD003PublicationDelays` |
| MD-004 | Bars are aligned to the session start and never cross a session boundary; a half day's last bar ends at the early close; extended-hours bars carry `session = pre/post` and are never merged with regular bars. Daily bars have `ts_open` = regular open and `ts_close` = regular close. 5m/15m/30m/1h bars are built from final 1-minute bars and must equal resampling the same minutes offline. | Implemented | `libs/go/calendar` (`BarOpens`, `BarClose`), `tbcore.data.calendar`, `live.Aggregate`; `TestDF011DSTAndHalfDays`, `TestMD004ResampledBarsEqualOracle` |
| MD-005 | Every service uses the XNYS session table generated from `exchange_calendars` (`scripts/gen_calendar.py`), embedded in Go and shipped in tbcore, mirrored to `refdata.sessions`; the Go and Python copies MUST be identical. | Implemented | `libs/go/calendar`, `tbcore.data.calendar`, `refdata.MirrorCalendar`; `test_calendar_matches_go_copy_and_handles_dst_half_days`, `test_calendar_is_fresh` |
| MD-006 | Gaps are detected against the session calendar, not by row count: after a stream reconnect every closed minute since the last final bar is fetched over REST and finalized exactly once (`repaired`), and the end-of-day backfill records runs of ≥ `TB_MD_MIN_GAP_RUN_1M` (5) missing regular-session minutes in `marketdata.gaps` and re-fetches them; an empty vendor answer for a gap stays recorded (a thinly traded name, not an error). Missed end-of-day jobs are re-run for sessions up to 72 h back, and every start queues a catch-up of the last `TB_MD_CATCHUP_DAYS` (10) days, so longer outages are filled too. | Implemented | `live.Pipeline.Repair`, `ingest.Backfiller` (gap pass, `SaveGap`), `sched.Scheduler.RunDue`, `cmd/marketdata` (`submitStartupJobs`); `TestMD006StreamKilledThreeMinutesIsRepairedOnce`, `TestMD001BackfillIdempotentWithGapRepairAndTickerChange`, `TestSchedulerRunsOncePerSessionIncludingHalfDays` |
| MD-007 | `IsFresh(instrument, timeframe, max_age)` MUST answer false (with a reason) when the stream is disconnected, the symbol is not subscribed, no final bar exists, or the last final bar closed longer ago than `max_age` + the finality window during a session; consumers (execution, RM-004) treat false as "block new risk". Stale subscribed symbols are exported as `tb_md_stale_symbols` and alert. | Implemented | `live.Pipeline.IsFresh`, `StaleSymbols`, `internal/api` (`IsFresh`); `TestMD007IsFresh`, `TestMD007IsFreshFailsClosedAndAllowlist` |
| MD-008 | Vendor calls respect the vendor's limits: FMP through a token bucket at 80% of the plan (`TB_FMP_RATE_PER_MIN`), a 30-day bandwidth budget, retries with jittered backoff honouring `Retry-After`, and a circuit breaker; only `/stable` endpoints are called; the API key never appears in errors or logs. Each source publishes `SourceHealth` on `sys.health.<source>` every 15 s. | Implemented | `internal/fmp`, `libs/go/resilience`, `cmd/marketdata` (`publishHealth`); `TestMD008RetryAfterBreakerAndLegacy`, `TestSE012APIKeyNeverInErrorsOrLogs` |
| MD-009 | Every vendor bar is keyed to an `instrument_id` through the point-in-time symbol map before storage; bars for a symbol unknown on that date are dropped and counted (`unknown`), never stored under a guessed instrument. | Implemented | `ingest.Normalizer.Normalize`, `refdata` (`Resolve`); `TestMD001BackfillIdempotentWithGapRepairAndTickerChange`, `TestDF006TickerChangesRecyclingAndDelisting` |
| MD-010 | Each morning, the previous sessions' Alpaca SIP daily bars are compared with FMP's non-split-adjusted EOD bars: close within 5 bps, high/low within 10 bps (or one tick each), and volume within 5% when both sides are consolidated (IEX volume is never compared with SIP). A breach quarantines that symbol-day in `marketdata.quarantine`, mirrored to `ref/quarantine.parquet`; feature materialization excludes quarantined symbol-days by default and research reads can opt in (`exclude_quarantined`). A bar missing on either side is recorded (`missing_primary` / `missing_secondary`) for the gap pass, not quarantined. | Implemented | `internal/recon` (`Compare`, `Reconciler.Run`), `tbcore.data.Lake.drop_quarantined`, `tbcore.features.materialize`; `TestMD010Tolerances`, `TestMD010BreachQuarantinesSymbolDay`, `TestMD010NonSplitAdjustedEODAndStableOnly`, `test_md010_quarantined_symbol_days_excluded` |
| MD-011 | A corporate action's `available_at` is its vendor declaration time when known, otherwise 00:00 New York on its ex-date, and never later than when the platform first fetched it; re-fetching keeps the earliest value. | Implemented | `services/marketdata/internal/refdata` (`AvailableAt`, `UpsertAction`); `TestMD011CorporateActionAvailability` |
| MD-012 | Corporate actions are fetched from Alpaca and FMP and cross-checked: same type, ex-date, and ratio within 1e-6 → `agreed`; a mismatch (including the same action with ex-dates up to 5 days apart) → `disagreed`, and adjusted reads for that instrument fail (`AdjustmentBlockedError`) until the owner marks the rows `confirmed` / `rejected` (kept across later syncs; `rejected` rows are ignored by adjustment); raw reads still work. Every stored action is published on `ref.corporate_action.<instrument_id>` (REF stream) for the ledger. | Implemented | `internal/refsync` (`CrossCheck`, `CorporateActions`), `tbcore.data.lake.apply_adjustment`; `TestMD012CrossCheckAgreementAndDisagreement`, `TestMD012NearMissExDatesDisagree`, `test_md012_disagreement_blocks_adjustment`, `test_md012_owner_rejected_action_is_ignored` |

## Schedule (New York time, trading sessions only)

| When | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 06:00 | Reference sync (assets, symbol changes, delistings, corporate actions ±30 days) and `ref/` export | MD-012, DF-006 |
| Close + 20 min (half days included) | Flush live bars, daily backfill of the session (source-final bars replace provisional ones as revisions) with gap pass, compaction, tick retention | MD-001, MD-006, ST-002, ST-008 |
| Service start | History load from `TB_MD_HISTORY_START` (once per universe) and catch-up of the last 10 days (once per day) | MD-001, MD-006 |
| 07:00 next morning | Reconciliation of the previous session against FMP EOD, quarantine export | MD-010 |

## Edge cases

| Situation | Behavior | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Stream drops for 3 minutes mid-session | On reconnect the missed closed minutes come from REST, each published once and flagged repaired; `IsFresh` is false while disconnected | MD-006, MD-007 |
| A minute bar arrives, then an `updatedBar` 30 s later, then another 2 minutes later | First update replaces the pending bar; the late one is a correction with a new revision | MD-002, DF-007 |
| No trades in a minute | No bar (Alpaca omits it); counts toward a gap only in runs ≥ 5 | MD-006 |
| Ticker recycled (old company delisted, new company reuses the ticker) | Requests carry `asof`; bars map to the instrument that held the ticker on that date | MD-001, MD-009, DF-006 |
| FMP returns 429 with `Retry-After: 10` | Waits 10 s, retries; repeated failures open the breaker and `sys.health.fmp` shows it | MD-008 |
| Alpaca and FMP disagree on a split ratio | Action `disagreed`; adjusted reads raise; owner resolves via the runbook | MD-012 |
| FMP EOD close 20 bps away from Alpaca | Symbol-day quarantined, excluded from feature snapshots, alert `ReconciliationBreaches` | MD-010 |
| Half day | Last bar ends at the early close; daily backfill runs at early close + 20 min | MD-004 |
