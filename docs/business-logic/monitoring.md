# Monitoring and alerting

**Prefix:** `MN-`  ·  **Owners:** `monitor`, Prometheus/Alertmanager (`infra/observability`), every service (metrics, bus alerts)  ·  **Related:** [risk](risk-management.md), [virtual portfolios](virtual-portfolios-and-attribution.md), [operations](operations.md), [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-observability-stack.md), [ADR-0027](../adr/0027-monitoring-ops-slice.md) (D45), [ADR-0044](../adr/0044-live-feedback-and-retraining-triggers.md) (D62), [ADR-0045](../adr/0045-full-monitoring-and-automatic-containment.md) (D63), [ADR-0046](../adr/0046-jetstream-recorder-for-live-and-paper-streams.md) (D64)

## Scope

Phase 2 ops slice of section 13: health and trading-operations alert rules, alert routing (severities, grouping, dedupe, escalation, quiet hours), the pre-market checklist and end-of-day summary, the trading dashboard, and the monitoring pipeline's own dead-man. Phase 4 adds the analyst feedback slice (MN-020..MN-026, [ADR-0044](../adr/0044-live-feedback-and-retraining-triggers.md)): live feedback reports, retraining triggers, and autonomy lowering. Phase 5 completes section 13 (MN-030..MN-046, ADR-0045, ADR-0046): performance against horizon-matched backtest bands, drawdown, drift, daily divergence on recorded sessions, implementation shortfall, a strategy health score, containment policies that demote or kill automatically through execution (RM-018), the alert inbox with acknowledgements and silences, graduation evidence for section 15, the JetStream recorder, and the Strategy overview and Model drift dashboards.

## Concepts

- **Two alert sources:** Prometheus rules on service metrics, and bus alerts (`alert.<severity>`) that services publish for events (breaks, halts, kill switches, dead-man). The monitor forwards bus alerts to Alertmanager, so one router handles both.
- **Health components** (MN-031): drawdown, performance, divergence, feature drift, prediction drift, execution quality, decay. Each has a warning and a breach threshold from the strategy's containment policy, a 0–100 score, and a minimum sample below which it is `insufficient data` and never acts.
- **Severities:** `info` (log only), `warning` (Pushover normal priority outside quiet hours), `critical` (Pushover emergency priority, repeats every 2 minutes until acknowledged, re-notified every 30 minutes while firing). `kind=report` alerts carry the daily reports (Pushover low priority). Every notifying receiver also sends Web Push to the installed PWA through api-gateway (SE-063).

## Edge cases

| Scenario | Expected behavior | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| broker-gateway stopped during market hours | `ServiceDown` (30 s) → Pushover emergency within about 45 s; execution pages "broker session not ready" after 10 s with strategies active | MN-004, RM-009 |
| Encroachment break | Ledger publishes `alert.critical` with the reconciliation runbook; Alertmanager pages; `LedgerReconciliationBroken` follows after 1 min | MN-002, VP-016 |
| Alertmanager down | Bus alerts stay unacknowledged and are retried every 10 s; critical risk paths still block trading | MN-002 |
| Same break re-alerted | Same `dedupe_key` label → one notification group | MN-002 |
| Warning at 23:00 | Muted until 07:00; visible in Alertmanager and Grafana | MN-003 |
| Paper strategy decays over 12 sessions (40 trades) | Report shows the CUSUM breach and below-p10; critical `RetrainSuggested`; analyst opens a live-feedback round at level 0 and the loop is paused | MN-023, MN-025, AN-013 |
| Decay visible after 3 sessions (12 trades) | Report published with `sample_sufficient = false`; no trigger | MN-024 |
| Paper model strategy loses 18 % over 3 sessions; backtest p95 drawdown over 3 sessions is 6 % | Drawdown 3× p95 → breach → `DemoteStrategy` → halted; critical alert with the evidence; nothing more until the owner acts | MN-032, MN-035, RM-018 |
| Live strategy breaches drawdown | live → paper (latched kill); the breach persists on paper, but the strategy is not demoted again to halted | MN-035 |
| Feature PSI 0.3 on the top feature | Warning alert "Feature drift warning", no action; at PSI 0.6 → breach → demote | MN-038, MN-031 |
| Large drift on a feature ranked 9 | Reported, never acts | MN-038 |
| Replay misses every AAPL intent (feed gap in the recording) | Match rate ≤ 0.8 over 5 sessions → breach → demote paper → halted | MN-037 |
| Match rate exactly 0.80 (the breach threshold) | Breach: a value equal to a threshold takes that threshold's status | MN-031, MN-037 |
| Recorder was down for an hour during the session | Manifest incomplete; divergence `insufficient data`; `MonitorRecordingIncomplete` warns | MN-037, MN-045 |
| Divergence breach and drawdown beyond the backtest p99 | Severe: latched strategy kill switch `cancel_open`, then demotion | MN-039 |
| Execution unreachable when containment decides | Action pending; critical "Containment action failed" and `MonitorContainmentPending`; retried every 15 s | MN-035 |
| Owner loosens the drawdown breach to 2.0× without a fresh passkey | Refused with step-up required; after the passkey, saved and audited with the loosened fields | MN-036 |
| Paper fills 12 bps better than modeled | Not a divergence (favourable gap); reported | MN-037 |
